## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Dupilumab blocks the IL-4 receptor alpha subunit, which inhibits both IL-4 and IL-13 signaling and reduces type 2 airway inflammation. Detailed mechanism-of-action data are not available in the data compiled for this report, so this description relies on the known pharmacology of the drug class.

Eosinophilic or type 2 airway diseases share this pathway. This includes type 2 COPD with a chronic bronchitis phenotype, where real-world case series and cohorts have reported dupilumab use. A review of novel therapies for eosinophilic pediatric plastic bronchitis is also among the retrieved literature.

However, the evidence is indirect. No cited trial or publication tests dupilumab in bronchitis as a defined disease. The link runs through related conditions (COPD, asthma, chronic rhinosinusitis), so the prediction should be treated as a research question rather than a supported use. Because no study directly addresses bronchitis, the evidence level is L5.
