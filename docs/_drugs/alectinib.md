## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available. Alectinib is an ALK-targeted therapy, and its efficacy in ALK-positive NSCLC is well established. Mechanistically, it would only be expected to help diseases driven by ALK signalling.

No ALK-driven mechanism has been established for hereditary gingival fibromatosis, a fibrous overgrowth of the gums. The very high TxGNN score (0.9997) reflects proximity in the knowledge graph rather than a demonstrated biological link. The link between the original and the predicted indication is therefore weak, and the prediction should be treated as a hypothesis only.
