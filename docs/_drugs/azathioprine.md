## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available. Azathioprine is a purine antimetabolite immunosuppressant, and its efficacy in transplant rejection and autoimmune disease is established. It is generally understood to suppress lymphocyte proliferation.

The predicted indication, colobomatous microphthalmia-rhizomelic dysplasia syndrome, is a rare congenital developmental disorder of the eye and skeleton. No plausible mechanistic link between purine antimetabolite immunosuppression and this condition has been identified. The very high score (99.99%) reflects the model's pattern matching in its knowledge graph and should not be read as biological or clinical support.

No trials or publications exist for this drug-disease pair, so the prediction is best treated as a hypothesis-generating signal only.
