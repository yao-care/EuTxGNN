# Trastuzumab emtansine: From HER2-Positive Breast Cancer to Progesterone-Receptor Negative Breast Cancer

## One-Sentence Summary

Trastuzumab emtansine (T-DM1) is a HER2-directed antibody-drug conjugate originally used for HER2-positive early and metastatic breast cancer.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **progesterone-receptor negative breast cancer**, supported by **1 clinical trial** (terminated) and **12 publications** that are mostly reviews, guidelines and case reports.
This is best read as a receptor-defined subgroup of the already approved HER2-positive population rather than a truly new indication.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on general knowledge, T-DM1 links the HER2-targeting antibody trastuzumab to the microtubule inhibitor DM1 (emtansine). The antibody binds HER2 on tumor cells, the conjugate is internalized, and DM1 is released inside the cell, killing it.

This mechanism depends on HER2, not on progesterone-receptor (PR) status. HER2-positive tumors can be PR-negative, so T-DM1 is plausibly active in that group. The same drug is already approved for HER2-positive breast cancer regardless of hormone-receptor status.

PR-negative disease is therefore largely covered by the current approved indication. The prediction is a subgroup of the approved population, not a distinct repurposing opportunity. For PR-negative tumors that are also HER2-negative, the mechanistic rationale is weak.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
- The prediction is a PR-negative subgroup of the already approved HER2-positive population, not a new indication. The only trial is terminated and mostly addresses chemotherapy de-escalation with other agents. The literature is reviews, guidelines and case reports.
- The data pipeline labeled this prediction L2 (Research Question), but the single terminated Phase 2 trial does not meet the completed Phase 2/3 RCT criterion. On a strict reading it is closer to L4.
- The EU authorization is cancelled, so there is no marketed product in the EU.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Clarification of why the EU authorization was cancelled (for example, a commercial withdrawal versus a regulatory decision).
- Mechanism of action data and SmPC safety information (warnings and contraindications), which are missing.
- Evidence on T-DM1 in PR-negative disease, stratified by HER2 status, to show whether any population falls outside the current label.
