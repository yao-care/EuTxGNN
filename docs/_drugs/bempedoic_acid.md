# Bempedoic acid: From Hypercholesterolaemia to Hyperthyroidism

## One-Sentence Summary

Bempedoic acid (marketed in the EU as Nilemdo) is a lipid-lowering drug for adults with primary hypercholesterolaemia, mixed dyslipidaemia, or established or high-risk atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Bempedoic acid is a non-statin cholesterol-lowering agent. It is understood to inhibit ATP-citrate lyase, an enzyme upstream of HMG-CoA reductase in cholesterol synthesis. Its efficacy in lowering LDL-C is established, but a mechanistic link to excess thyroid hormone is not apparent.

The high score (99.61%) is a graph-based prediction only. Hyperthyroidism is an endocrine disorder of thyroid hormone excess and is unrelated to the original lipid indication. Nothing in the available data connects ATP-citrate lyase inhibition to thyroid hormone production or action. The prediction should therefore be read as a hypothesis from the knowledge graph, not as a finding with biological support.

Among the other predicted indications, **homozygous familial hypercholesterolaemia** (score 99.48%) has the most supporting evidence. It includes a real-world cohort study in HoFH patients (PMID 41274797) and several reviews. It is closely related to the approved indications and would be an extension of them rather than a true repurposing. If the goal is to prioritise candidates with real-world support, it deserves a separate evaluation.
