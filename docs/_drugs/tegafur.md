# Tegafur: From Gastric Cancer to Colonic Neoplasm

## One-Sentence Summary

Tegafur is an oral fluoropyrimidine prodrug of 5-fluorouracil (5-FU), historically used alone or as a component of combination regimens (UFT, S-1) for gastrointestinal cancers.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **Colonic Neoplasm**,

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available. Based on known information, Tegafur is an oral prodrug of 5-fluorouracil (5-FU), a fluoropyrimidine antimetabolite that inhibits thymidylate synthase and disrupts DNA/RNA synthesis in rapidly dividing cells. It is commonly used as a component of combination regimens such as UFT (tegafur + uracil) and S-1 (tegafur + gimeracil + oteracil), which pair tegafur with agents that modulate 5-FU metabolism to improve efficacy and reduce gastrointestinal toxicity.

The literature evidence in this pack shows that tegafur-based regimens (UFT, S-1/TS-1) have already been extensively studied and used clinically for colorectal and colon cancer (e.g., adjuvant therapy for Stage II/III colon cancer, metastatic colorectal cancer), alongside their traditional use in gastric cancer. This means the "new" indication predicted by TxGNN — colonic neoplasm — substantially overlaps with an indication that is already well-established for tegafur-based combination products in several markets (notably Japan), even though a standalone EU authorization for Tegafur itself is not present in this dataset.

Mechanistically, gastric and colorectal cancers are both gastrointestinal adenocarcinomas that share similar sensitivity to fluoropyrimidine-based antimetabolite chemotherapy, which explains why a drug developed and validated for gastric cancer would show strong knowledge-graph proximity to colonic neoplasm. The very large volume of Phase 3 randomized trial data (UFT/LV, TS-1, S-1 regimens) for colon and colorectal cancer strongly corroborates this mechanistic plausibility, independent of the TxGNN score itself.

## EU Market Information

Tegafur currently holds **no marketing authorisation** in the EU as a standalone active substance (0 authorizations recorded). It is only available clinically as part of fixed-dose combination products (e.g., UFT, S-1/Teysuno), which are tracked separately and are not covered by this Evidence Pack.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
- The clinical trial and literature evidence for tegafur-based regimens in colon/colorectal cancer is strong (L1: multiple completed Phase 3 RCTs), but Tegafur has no EU marketing authorization (0 licenses), and a **Blocking** data gap on TFDA/SmPC warnings and contraindications currently prevents the mandatory S1 safety pre-assessment.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- TFDA/SmPC label data (warnings, contraindications) — download and parse the official product label PDF
- Detailed mechanism of action (MOA) data via DrugBank API query
- Clarification of Tegafur's regulatory status as a standalone substance vs. its role in combination products (UFT, S-1) authorized in the EU
- A DPD (DPYD) deficiency screening protocol, given the well-documented fluoropyrimidine toxicity risk highlighted in the trial evidence
