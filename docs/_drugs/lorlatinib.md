## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on known information, lorlatinib is a brain-penetrant ALK/ROS1 tyrosine kinase inhibitor. Its efficacy in ALK-positive NSCLC is well established, including in the Phase 3 CROWN trial.

For gingival fibromatosis, no ALK-driven mechanism has been established. The high score (99.81%) is a computational prediction only. At present, there is no biological or clinical basis to link ALK inhibition to this condition, so the prediction should be treated as a hypothesis-generating signal rather than a credible repurposing candidate.
