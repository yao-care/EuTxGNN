## Safety Considerations

No label-based warnings, contraindications, or drug interaction data were available. Please refer to the SmPC for safety information.

From general knowledge of the drug class, ARIA and infusion-related reactions are recognized risks of anti-amyloid antibodies. They would need careful weighing in any non-neurological use.
