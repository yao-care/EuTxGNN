## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on known information, eftrenonacog alfa is a recombinant factor IX-Fc fusion protein, which supplements factor IX in the intrinsic coagulation cascade.

Pseudo-von Willebrand disease is a platelet-type bleeding disorder caused by a gain-of-function defect in GP1BA, which increases platelet affinity for von Willebrand factor. Factor IX replacement does not address this defect, so the mechanistic link is weak. The high TxGNN score is more likely a network-proximity artifact, for example from shared bleeding-disorder annotations, than evidence of real therapeutic benefit.
