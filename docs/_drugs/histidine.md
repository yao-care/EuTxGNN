## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available. Histidine is an essential amino acid and the precursor of histamine, which is made through the enzyme L-histidine decarboxylase. Histamine takes part in regulating gastric secretion and may influence gut motility, so a link to gastric function is conceivable in principle.

This link is speculative. The only publication retrieved (PMID 42668409) describes gastric amyloid pathology and loss of nerve fibres in the stomach lining in hereditary ATTR amyloidosis. It does not test histidine and does not study gastroparesis treatment. No study directly addresses histidine in gastroparesis, and the high TxGNN score alone is not evidence of benefit.
