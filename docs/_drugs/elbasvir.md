## One-Sentence Summary

Elbasvir is a hepatitis C virus (HCV) NS5A inhibitor used as part of a fixed-dose combination with grazoprevir.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **hepatitis B virus infection**, but no study tests elbasvir in hepatitis B.
The data compiled for this report contain **2 clinical trials** and **20 publications** linked to this prediction, all of which concern hepatitis C or general viral hepatitis, so the evidence level is **L5**.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data are not available in the data compiled for this report. Elbasvir is known as an HCV NS5A inhibitor, and it is used together with the NS3/4A protease inhibitor grazoprevir.

A direct mechanistic link to hepatitis B is not established. Hepatitis B virus has no NS5A homolog, so the target of elbasvir has no counterpart in this virus. The high TxGNN score probably reflects network proximity to other hepatitis viruses rather than a biological rationale.

Both linked trials enrolled HCV patients, not HBV patients. The literature is mostly HCV reviews and real-world HCV reports, with no HBV-specific efficacy data for elbasvir. This prediction should therefore be treated as a hypothesis only.
