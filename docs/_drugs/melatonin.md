## Safety Considerations

**Contraindications (from the knowledge graph, not label text):** The TxGNN knowledge graph links melatonin to contraindication with several conditions, including liver failure and liver disease, kidney disease, autoimmune disease (including systemic lupus erythematosus), psoriasis, sarcoidosis, anxiety and neurotic disorders, schizophrenia, substance abuse/dependence, and nephrolithiasis. These are database associations and should be verified against the product information.

**Drug Interactions:** 17 database-listed interactions were found.
- **Moderate:** deferasirox, warfarin, alpelisib, anagrelide, lorlatinib, olaparib, osimertinib, rucaparib, teriflunomide, vemurafenib, ifosfamide.
- **Minor:** cimetidine, obeticholic acid, caffeine, osilodrostat, ethinylestradiol, diethylstilbestrol.

Warfarin deserves particular attention in practice. Sedation is also a consideration, and this should be monitored in any migraine trial.

Please refer to the SmPC for label warnings and contraindications.
