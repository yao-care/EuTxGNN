## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on known information, liraglutide belongs to the GLP-1 receptor agonist class. Its efficacy in type 2 diabetes and weight management is established, and the predicted link to opsismodysplasia is not established.

Opsismodysplasia is a rare skeletal dysplasia caused by INPPL1 mutations, which cause SHIP2 deficiency. No direct mechanistic link to liraglutide has been identified. The only conceivable connection is a theoretical one through insulin/PI3K signalling, and it is speculative.
