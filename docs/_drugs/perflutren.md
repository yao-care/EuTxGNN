## Safety Considerations

- **Drug Interactions**: 100 interactions were identified in total, and the first 20 returned are summarized below.
  - **Major**: dolasetron, cisapride
  - **Moderate**: loperamide, bisacodyl, clarithromycin, picosulfuric acid, polyethylene glycol (3350 with electrolytes), palonosetron, sodium sulfate, levofloxacin, lactitol, lactulose, ondansetron, castor oil, glycerin, granisetron, magnesium citrate, magnesium hydroxide, mineral oil
  - **Minor**: metronidazole
- **Other signals from the literature compiled for this report**: perflutren agents carry a US black-box warning based on serious cardiopulmonary events, and rare adverse events such as new-onset seizure have been reported. These come from studies in cardiac imaging, not from the predicted indication.

For label-level warnings and contraindications, please refer to the SmPC.
