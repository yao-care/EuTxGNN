## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on known pharmacology, migalastat is an iminosugar that binds the active site of alpha-galactosidase A (alpha-Gal A) and stabilizes amenable mutant forms of the enzyme. This helps the enzyme fold correctly and reach the lysosome.

No mechanistic link to portal vein thrombosis has been identified. Portal vein thrombosis is a vascular and haematological condition that does not depend on alpha-Gal A folding. The score of 98.85% is shared identically with several other hepatic and portal hypertension predictions (hepatoportal sclerosis, hepatopulmonary syndrome, early-onset familial noncirrhotic portal hypertension and idiopathic copper-associated cirrhosis). This suggests the score reflects a shared neighbourhood in the knowledge graph rather than a disease-specific signal. The prediction should be treated as a hypothesis-generating output only.

The other top predictions (for example hepatic porphyria and nodular regenerative hyperplasia of the liver) also lack an identified mechanism.
