## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Riociguat is known to be a soluble guanylate cyclase (sGC) stimulator. It enhances cGMP signaling, which relaxes pulmonary blood vessels and reduces vessel remodeling. This has been proven in CTEPH and PAH.

The link to the predicted disease is weak. A connection between sGC/cGMP signaling and hair follicle biology is plausible but speculative. Ambras syndrome is a rare congenital disorder with a distinct genetic basis, a chromosomal rearrangement that alters regulation of the *TRPS1* gene. A drug acting on vascular signaling is not expected to correct this. The high score (0.949) is a graph-based prediction, and no clinical or literature evidence supports it.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The prediction has no clinical trials and no publications. The proposed mechanism is speculative, and the disease is a genetically determined developmental disorder unlikely to respond to sGC stimulation. The only EU authorization is also cancelled.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**

- Detailed mechanism of action data
- Warnings and contraindications from the SmPC, to complete safety screening
