## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Benralizumab binds the IL-5 receptor alpha subunit (IL5RA) and depletes eosinophils and basophils through antibody-dependent cell-mediated cytotoxicity. Detailed mechanism-of-action data are not otherwise available in the data compiled for this report.

For immune-mediated platelet destruction, no established mechanistic link to IL-5 receptor blockade is supported by the data. Immune thrombocytopenia is mainly driven by autoantibody-mediated platelet clearance and impaired platelet production, not by eosinophil or basophil pathways. The high score (0.9934) is a model output and should not be read as evidence of efficacy.
