## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Benralizumab binds the IL-5 receptor alpha subunit (IL5RA) and depletes eosinophils and basophils through antibody-dependent cell-mediated cytotoxicity. According to the Mechanism of Action section of the US FDA-approved prescribing information for benralizumab: "Benralizumab is a humanized afucosylated, monoclonal antibody (IgG1, kappa) that directly binds to the alpha subunit of the human interleukin-5 receptor (IL-5Rα) with a dissociation constant of 11 pM. The IL-5 receptor is expressed on the surface of eosinophils and basophils." ([DailyMed: Fasenra (benralizumab) prescribing information, AstraZeneca Pharmaceuticals LP](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=da6aca1a-19ed-44a4-abb7-696c7d58b784), label version 2026-05-13; accessed 2026-11-20.)

For immune-mediated platelet destruction, no established mechanistic link to IL-5 receptor blockade is supported by the data. Immune thrombocytopenia is mainly driven by autoantibody-mediated platelet clearance and impaired platelet production, not by eosinophil or basophil pathways. The high score (0.9934) is a model output and should not be read as evidence of efficacy.
