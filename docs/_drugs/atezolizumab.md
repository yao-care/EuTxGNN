## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Atezolizumab is generally known to be an antibody that blocks PD-L1, which restores T-cell activity against tumours. It has proven efficacy in several PD-L1-expressing cancers, so it may be mechanistically applicable to other tumours with immune infiltration.

The predicted disease is an adenocarcinoma of the female genital tract. PD-L1 blockade could plausibly act on such tumours, but this is speculative for this specific entity. The prediction comes from the model only. No study in the compiled data tests atezolizumab in uterine ligament adenocarcinoma.
