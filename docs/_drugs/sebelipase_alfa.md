## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Sebelipase alfa is a recombinant human lysosomal acid lipase. It replaces the deficient enzyme in LAL deficiency, which reduces the lysosomal build-up of cholesteryl esters and triglycerides.

The biological link to Scheie syndrome is weak. Scheie syndrome is caused by a deficiency of alpha-L-iduronidase, a different enzyme that acts on different substrates. Sebelipase alfa does not replace it, so there is no overlap in enzyme or substrate. The high score most likely reflects the shared "lysosomal storage disease" neighbourhood in the knowledge graph rather than a real therapeutic connection. The prediction should therefore be treated as a model artefact until shown otherwise.

**Context from other predictions:** The best-supported prediction for this drug is *cholesteryl ester storage disease* (rank 4, TxGNN score 99.72%), the later-onset form of LAL deficiency. It is already covered by the approved indication, so it is not true repurposing. It is backed by a pivotal Phase 3 placebo-controlled RCT (NCT01757184, n=66; NEJM 2015, PMID 26352813), several Phase 2 studies, long-term extension data and an expanded access program. Wolman disease (rank 5), the severe infant form of LAL deficiency, is also within the approved indication and is supported by small, uncontrolled infant cohorts.
