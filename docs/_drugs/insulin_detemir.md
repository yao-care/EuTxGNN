## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

According to the Mechanism of Action section of the US FDA-approved prescribing information for insulin detemir: "The primary activity of insulin, including LEVEMIR, is regulation of glucose metabolism. Insulins and its analogs lower blood glucose by stimulating peripheral glucose uptake, especially by skeletal muscle and fat, and by inhibiting hepatic glucose production." ([DailyMed: Levemir (insulin detemir) prescribing information, A-S Medication Solutions](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=82192527-99aa-4b53-8ce9-9173668d309c), label version 2024-10-09; accessed 2026-11-19.) Insulin detemir is an insulin analogue that acts on insulin receptors to lower blood glucose.

Autoimmune oophoritis is an immune-mediated inflammatory disorder of the ovary. No clear mechanistic rationale links insulin to this condition. The connection is speculative, and the high model score reflects a computational association only, not biological or clinical evidence. A score on its own does not show that the drug would work, so this prediction should be treated as a hypothesis to examine, not a lead to act on.
