# Alpelisib: From HR+/HER2- PIK3CA-Mutated Breast Cancer to Pulmonary Hypertension

## One-Sentence Summary

Alpelisib is a PI3Kα inhibitor, originally used together with fulvestrant for advanced breast cancer with a PIK3CA mutation.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **pulmonary hypertension**, but **no clinical trials** and only **1 publication** (a case report of alpelisib-induced lung disease, which is an adverse event rather than a benefit) are linked to this prediction.
This is a model-only prediction without supporting clinical evidence.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on known information, alpelisib is a PI3Kα inhibitor whose efficacy in PIK3CA-mutated breast cancer has been shown in a Phase 3 trial (SOLAR-1), and mechanistically it may be applicable to diseases driven by PI3K signaling.

PI3K/AKT/mTOR signaling has been implicated in pulmonary vascular remodeling, which gives a theoretical reason for the prediction. However, the relationship between breast cancer and pulmonary hypertension is indirect, and the model score is not supported by any clinical or literature signal of benefit.

The only linked paper reports interstitial lung disease caused by alpelisib. This is a pulmonary toxicity signal and argues for caution rather than support.

Other predictions in the list were also reviewed. Most (migraine entries, kyphoscoliotic heart disease, leprosy, amyotrophic lateral sclerosis) have no evidence at all. Thrombotic disease has indirect support from reviews of PI3K inhibitors in vascular malformations (rated as a research question), and rheumatoid arthritis has only a general review. Multiple endocrine neoplasia is linked to four trials that are all in breast or ovarian cancer and appear to be matching artifacts. These do not change the recommendation for the top-ranked prediction.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The TxGNN score is very high (0.990), but the prediction has no supporting clinical trials, and the only linked paper describes lung toxicity from alpelisib. The Piqray EU authorization is also cancelled, and warnings and contraindications data are not available.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Preclinical evidence that PI3Kα inhibition benefits pulmonary vascular remodeling, with attention to the direction of effect
- Evaluation of the interstitial lung disease risk in the context of pulmonary disease
- Mechanism of action data and SmPC warnings and contraindications
- Clarification of why the EU authorization was cancelled and of current availability
