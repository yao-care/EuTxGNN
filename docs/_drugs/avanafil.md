## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on known pharmacology, avanafil is a selective PDE5 inhibitor. It raises cGMP levels and promotes vasodilation, which is the basis of its effect in erectile dysfunction.

A theoretical link to uterine or ovarian blood flow exists. However, amenorrhea has diverse hormonal and structural causes (for example hypothalamic, pituitary, ovarian and uterine causes), and PDE5 inhibition does not address most of them. The high TxGNN score is a computational prediction with no supporting trials or literature, so the mechanistic rationale remains weak.

Other candidates in the predicted list are mechanistically more plausible, notably **Raynaud disease** (rank 5, score 88.5%) and **stroke disorder** (rank 9, score 79.2%). Both are flagged as research questions because vasodilation via cGMP and class-level data for other PDE5 inhibitors support them. Several other predictions (such as bovine rhinotracheitis and malignant catarrh, which are veterinary diseases) look like knowledge-graph artifacts.
