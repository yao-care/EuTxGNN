# Panobinostat: From Multiple Myeloma to Myeloid Leukemia

## One-Sentence Summary

Panobinostat is an oral histone deacetylase (HDAC) inhibitor, originally authorised in the EU for relapsed and/or refractory multiple myeloma in combination with bortezomib and dexamethasone.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **myeloid leukemia**, with **17 clinical trials** and **20 publications** compiled for this direction. Most trials are early-phase, and the only Phase 3 trial was terminated early.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism-of-action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Panobinostat is, however, a well-known pan-HDAC inhibitor. In myeloid leukemia, especially acute myeloid leukemia (AML), abnormal epigenetic silencing blocks cell differentiation. Oncogenic fusion proteins such as AML1-ETO and PML-RARA recruit HDACs to do this. Inhibiting HDACs can restore histone acetylation, reactivate tumour-suppressor genes, and induce differentiation and cell death.

Multiple myeloma and myeloid leukemia are both haematological malignancies in which epigenetic dysregulation plays a role. Preclinical work supports this link:
- Panobinostat drives plasmacytoid dendritic cell activation and IFNAR-dependent differentiation in AML models.
- It is active in bortezomib-resistant AML models.
- It shows synergy with hypomethylating agents (azacitidine), cytotoxic chemotherapy, and other targeted agents.

The TxGNN score is a computational prediction only and was not treated as clinical evidence.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The evidence is L2: several completed Phase 1/2 trials of panobinostat in AML/MDS, but no completed randomised Phase 3 evidence. The only Phase 3 trial (NCT04326764) was terminated after 52 patients. The EU authorisation has been cancelled, so no marketed product is currently available. The label safety data (warnings and contraindications) could not be retrieved, so safety screening cannot proceed. The high TxGNN score (99.70%) is a prediction only and does not change this assessment.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Retrieve and parse the SmPC (warnings and contraindications) to complete safety screening
- Obtain mechanism-of-action data from DrugBank
- Check why NCT04326764 was terminated and whether any results were reported for it and the completed Phase 2 trials (e.g. NCT00946647, NCT00880269)
- Confirm the regulatory pathway and product availability, given the cancelled EU authorisation
- Assess the QT and drug-interaction risks of combination regimens with standard AML chemotherapy and supportive care
