## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Clascoterone (Winlevi) is a topical anti-androgen. The data compiled for this report show that it targets the androgen receptor (AR), and it is understood to act locally in the sebaceous glands. Detailed mechanism of action data are not available in the compiled data beyond this target information.

Acne vulgaris is an androgen-driven sebaceous and follicular condition, whereas candidiasis is a fungal infection. No antifungal mechanism is known for clascoterone, and the data show no biological link between androgen receptor blockade and *Candida* control. The high TxGNN score (0.948) is most likely explained by network proximity to skin or mucosal disease nodes in the knowledge graph rather than by a biological rationale.

For context, other top-ranked predictions (migraine disorders, leprosy, oral candidiasis, adrenal gland hyperfunction, multiple endocrine neoplasia and others) also lack mechanistic or clinical support in the compiled data. Several of them, such as the migraine subtypes, appear to reflect the same graph association rather than independent evidence. The prediction for candidiasis should therefore be treated as a hypothesis only.

## Safety Considerations

- **Drug Interactions / Pharmacology**: The only interaction record is pharmacological. Clascoterone binds the human androgen receptor (AR), which is its intended target. No drug-drug interactions with other medicines were identified.
- **Other considerations**: Clascoterone has been associated with HPA axis suppression in some patients. This would be a particular concern in any endocrine-related use.

Please refer to the SmPC for full warnings and contraindications.
