## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available (flagged as a High-severity data gap, DG002). Based on the trial context included in this evidence pack, Roxadustat (FG-4592) is a HIF prolyl-hydroxylase inhibitor (HIF-PHI) referenced as a treatment for renal anemia in chronic kidney disease, alongside erythropoietin (EPO). No formal EU marketing authorization or approved indication text is on file for this product.

The single piece of supporting evidence, NCT06287879, is an observational (non-interventional) study examining meibomian gland structure and function in renal anemia patients who also present with dry eye symptoms — it does **not** test whether Roxadustat treats dry eye syndrome. The trial's own relevance grading (Grade C) confirms this is an indirect association only: patients on Roxadustat for their kidney disease happened to be evaluated for a co-occurring eye condition.

Mechanistically, the rationale proposed is that the HIF pathway may play a role in hypoxia-related regulation of lacrimal and meibomian gland function, which could theoretically link HIF stabilization to tear-film/ocular surface biology. However, this is a theoretical extrapolation with no causal or interventional evidence — the repurposing rationale itself explicitly states "無因果證據" (no causal evidence).

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The only supporting evidence is a single, low-relevance observational trial (Grade C) that does not directly test Roxadustat's efficacy for dry eye syndrome, and no literature evidence exists. Combined with blocking data gaps in mechanism of action and product safety information, this candidate does not currently meet the evidence bar to proceed past initial screening (Decision Stage S0).

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Mechanism of action (MOA) data from DrugBank (DG002)
- Official product warnings/contraindications from a regulatory source (DG001) — required before any safety (S1) evaluation
- An interventional/efficacy trial directly testing Roxadustat in dry eye syndrome patients (current evidence is purely observational and in a different patient population context)
- Clarification of regulatory status, since the drug is not currently marketed in the EU
