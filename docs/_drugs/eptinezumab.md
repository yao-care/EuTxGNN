## Safety Considerations

- **Drug Interactions**: No interaction records were found in the data compiled for this report.
- **Emerging safety signals (class-level, from recent cohort studies)**: Studies of CGRP inhibitors have examined glaucoma risk and cerebrovascular risk (ischemic stroke/TIA) in people with migraine. These are observational safety questions that deserve attention when considering use in patients with aura, and they are not a basis for conclusions here.
