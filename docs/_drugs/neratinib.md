## Safety Considerations

- **Drug Interactions**: The interaction screening returned 100 interacting drugs. Examples of the ones flagged as Major:
  - Acid-suppressing agents: famotidine, ranitidine, cimetidine, and the proton pump inhibitors omeprazole, esomeprazole, lansoprazole, dexlansoprazole and rabeprazole
  - Aprepitant
  - Dexamethasone
  - Clarithromycin
  - Clotrimazole

  Moderate-level interactions include loperamide (relevant because it is used for neratinib-associated diarrhoea), magnesium oxide, aluminium hydroxide, calcium carbonate, empagliflozin and naldemedine. Minor interactions include naloxegol and fidaxomicin.
