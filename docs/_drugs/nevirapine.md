# Nevirapine: From HIV-1 Infection to Simian Immunodeficiency Virus Infection

## One-Sentence Summary

Nevirapine is a non-nucleoside reverse transcriptase inhibitor (NNRTI), originally authorised in the EU for HIV-1 infection in combination with other antiretrovirals.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **simian immunodeficiency virus (SIV) infection**, but this is essentially an animal-model indication, supported by **0 clinical trials** and **14 laboratory, animal-model or review publications**. The published work also suggests that wild-type SIV is largely insensitive to NNRTIs.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Nevirapine is known as an HIV-1-specific NNRTI. It binds an allosteric pocket on HIV-1 reverse transcriptase and blocks viral DNA synthesis. SIV is a close lentiviral relative of HIV, which likely explains why the model scores this link so highly.

Biology limits how far this reasoning goes. Published work indicates that wild-type SIV and HIV-2 are intrinsically resistant to most NNRTIs. The reason is differences in the NNRTI-binding pocket of their reverse transcriptase. SIV/HIV-1 chimeric viruses (RT-SHIV) carry the HIV-1 reverse transcriptase gene and are sensitive to nevirapine. They are therefore used as preclinical models to study NNRTI efficacy and resistance in macaques.

The prediction mainly reflects research-model use, not a treatable human disease. It has no direct human clinical relevance beyond that model use.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
- The SIV prediction rests only on in vitro, animal-model and review literature, with no clinical trials. Wild-type SIV is largely resistant to NNRTIs, and SIV is not a human disease target.
- The only EU authorisation is cancelled, and nevirapine carries significant hepatic and skin toxicity concerns.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- A defined purpose for the SIV link, namely whether it is a research-model use or a veterinary indication, and not a human therapeutic target.
- Direct nevirapine susceptibility data for the relevant SIV strains and for RT-SHIV models.
- Detailed mechanism of action data and the current SmPC safety sections (warnings and contraindications).
- If human use is the real interest, a review of the other higher-ranked predictions. The perinatal and pediatric HIV and AIDS-related complex predictions fall within nevirapine's existing HIV indication and are not novel repurposing.
