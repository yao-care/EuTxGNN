## Safety Considerations

**Drug Interactions**: The interaction screen returned 100 interacting drugs in total. The first 20 entries reviewed include:
- **Major**: clarithromycin, dolasetron, palonosetron
- **Moderate**: several antidiabetic agents (acarbose, metformin, pioglitazone, alogliptin, linagliptin, saxagliptin, canagliflozin, dapagliflozin, empagliflozin, chlorpropamide, albiglutide, dulaglutide), famotidine, loperamide, bisacodyl, picosulfuric acid, and polyethylene glycol (3350 with electrolytes)

The many antidiabetic interactions are relevant because blood glucose control needs attention when this drug is used with them. Please refer to the SmPC for warnings and contraindications.
