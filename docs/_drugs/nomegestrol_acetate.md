## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available. Based on known information, nomegestrol acetate is a progestin used in combined oral contraceptives, and its efficacy in contraception is established. No plausible mechanism links it to antifungal activity.

The prediction appears to come from the knowledge-graph model rather than from any demonstrated pharmacology. If anything, progestogens and hormonal exposure have been associated with altered susceptibility to vulvovaginal candidiasis, so a therapeutic benefit is not supported. Without MOA data, no mechanistic rationale can be built.

Other top-ranked predictions show the same pattern. They include antithrombin deficiency type 2, heparin cofactor 2 deficiency, factor 5 excess with spontaneous thrombosis, plasma cell myeloma, gout, indolent plasma cell myeloma, heart neoplasm, thrombotic disease and rheumatoid arthritis. All are L5 (prediction only), except thrombotic disease at L4. The thrombosis-related predictions more plausibly reflect a safety signal, namely venous thromboembolism risk with hormonal therapy, than a therapeutic opportunity.
