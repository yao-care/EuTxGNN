# Emtricitabine: From HIV-1 Infection to Simian Immunodeficiency Virus Infection

## One-Sentence Summary

Emtricitabine is a nucleoside reverse transcriptase inhibitor originally used, together with other antiretroviral agents, to treat HIV-1 infection in adults and children.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **simian immunodeficiency virus (SIV) infection**, but there are **no registered clinical trials**, and the supporting evidence consists of **about 20 publications**, almost all from animal studies.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not currently available. Based on known information, emtricitabine is a nucleoside reverse transcriptase inhibitor (NRTI) that is active against the reverse transcriptase of lentiviruses. Its efficacy in HIV-1 infection is established, and mechanistically it may be applicable to SIV infection.

SIV is the monkey counterpart of HIV and is closely related to it. In macaque studies, emtricitabine combined with tenofovir (TDF or TAF) protected animals from repeated rectal and vaginal challenge with SIV/SHIV. These findings support biological plausibility.

However, SIV infection is an animal disease, not a human indication. The value of this prediction is mainly as a translational model for HIV prevention and treatment research, and it does not suggest a new human use. Other candidates in the prediction list are discussed below.

## Safety Considerations

- **Drug Interactions**: 20 interactions were identified. The two rated Major are **leflunomide** and **teriflunomide**. Most of the others are rated Moderate, including orlistat, naltrexone, cobicistat, interferon beta-1a/1b, peginterferon beta-1a, methotrexate, cladribine, clofarabine, epirubicin, idelalisib, cabozantinib, brentuximab vedotin, asparaginase, pegaspargase, tioguanine and trabectedin. - **Knowledge-graph contraindication signals** (not label text, so to be verified against the SmPC): liver disease and liver failure, kidney disease, Fanconi renotubular syndrome, lactic acidosis, fatty liver disease/non-alcoholic steatohepatitis, chronic hepatitis B and C virus infection, hypophosphatemia/osteomalacia/rickets, long QT syndrome/torsades de pointes, and several psychiatric and neurological conditions.

Label warnings and contraindications were not available in the data compiled for this report. Please refer to the SmPC for confirmed safety information.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The top prediction, SIV infection, is an animal disease supported only by preclinical macaque studies, with no registered clinical trials, so it is not a human repurposing opportunity. The most plausible human-relevant candidates (AIDS related complex and congenital HIV) overlap the approved HIV-1 indication. The only EU authorization (Emtriva) is cancelled.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Clarify whether the objective is a human indication or use of SIV/SHIV models as a translational proxy for HIV prevention research
- Confirm the current regulatory status of emtricitabine in the EU (for example, via fixed-dose combination products) and obtain the SmPC warnings and contraindications
- Detailed mechanism of action data from DrugBank
- Review of the Major interactions (leflunomide, teriflunomide) and the renal, hepatic and hepatitis B-related safety signals before any new use
