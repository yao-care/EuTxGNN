## Safety Considerations

- **Disease contraindication signals from the knowledge graph** (these are graph-derived associations, not label text): kidney disease, liver disease, gallbladder disease, pituitary hormone deficiencies (including hypopituitarism and Sheehan syndrome), hypogonadotropic hypogonadism (including Kallmann syndrome), adrenocortical insufficiency (Addison disease), thymus gland disease, infectious disease, and substance abuse/dependence.
- **Drug Interactions**: 100 interactions were found in total, and 20 are listed in the data compiled for this report. Most are moderate. Examples include phenylephrine, pseudoephedrine, epinephrine, salbutamol, formoterol, hydrocortisone, hydrochlorothiazide, acetazolamide, nifedipine, amiodarone, benazepril, estradiol, ethinylestradiol, levonorgestrel, alpelisib, amobarbital and ethanol. Topical hydrocortisone and topical alclometasone are minor.

Please refer to the SmPC for the official warnings and contraindications.
