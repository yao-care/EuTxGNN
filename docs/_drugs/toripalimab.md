## Safety Considerations

- **Immune-related adverse events**: Published reports link PD-1/PD-L1 inhibitors, including toripalimab, to severe skin reactions (Stevens-Johnson syndrome/toxic epidermal necrolysis) and lichenoid drug eruption. Immune checkpoint inhibitors have also been reported to cause autoimmune hemolytic anemia, immune-mediated marrow toxicity, and myositis or dermatomyositis-like syndromes. These adverse events overlap directly with several predicted indications.
- **Neonatal population**: No pediatric or neonatal data are available for toripalimab.

For the full label warnings, contraindications and interactions, please refer to the SmPC.
