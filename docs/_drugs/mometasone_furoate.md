## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available. Based on known information, mometasone furoate is a topical/inhaled corticosteroid with anti-inflammatory and immunosuppressive activity. Its efficacy in asthma has been established, and mechanistically it could plausibly modulate allergic contact sensitization.

However, this link is only a plausible hypothesis. The high TxGNN score is not backed by any clinical trial or published study, so the prediction should be treated as a model-generated lead rather than a validated finding.
