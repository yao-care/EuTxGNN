## Safety Considerations

- **Drug Interactions**: 14 interaction records were found.
  - **Major**: lenalidomide, pomalidomide, thalidomide.
  - **Moderate**: corticosteroids (hydrocortisone, triamcinolone, dexamethasone, betamethasone, budesonide, prednisolone, prednisone, methylprednisolone, deflazacort) and idelalisib.
- **Immune-related adverse events (from the literature)**: Case reports describe fatal or fulminant myocarditis with checkpoint inhibitors, capillary leak syndrome, bullous pemphigoid, pulmonary sarcoid-like granulomatosis, and vitiligo-like depigmentation. A series of 79 cases reported severe late-onset grade III-IV adverse events.

Please refer to the SmPC for formal warnings and contraindications.
