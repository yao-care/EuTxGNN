## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data are not available in the data compiled for this report. Ofatumumab is a human anti-CD20 monoclonal antibody. It binds a distinct epitope close to the cell membrane and kills B cells through complement-dependent cytotoxicity (CDC) and antibody-dependent cellular cytotoxicity (ADCC).

The predicted indication is a molecular subtype of CLL/SLL, characterized by unmutated IGHV. Because the malignant B cells in CLL/SLL express CD20, there is a plausible mechanistic basis for ofatumumab. The link to the original indication is that both multiple sclerosis and CLL/SLL involve CD20-positive B cells as the therapeutic target.

This is only a mechanistic inference. No trials or publications address this specific subtype. Any supporting evidence comes indirectly from the parent CLL/SLL disease.

**Context from related predictions:** The data compiled for this report show considerably more evidence for neighboring entries. The broader CLL/SLL entry has multiple Phase 2 trials and Phase 3 trials in which ofatumumab was the comparator (for example NCT02004522 and NCT01578707). Follicular lymphoma has several Phase 2 trials. These do not count as evidence for this subtype, but they suggest that any development work should be assessed under the broader CLL/SLL indication.

## Safety Considerations

- **Drug Interactions**: 80 interactions were identified in total, and 20 are detailed in the data compiled for this report.
  - **Major**: deferiprone, samarium (153Sm) lexidronam, adalimumab, baricitinib, certolizumab pegol, cladribine, clozapine.
  - **Moderate** (examples): other immunosuppressive or biologic agents (alemtuzumab, azathioprine, anakinra, canakinumab, alefacept), live and inactivated vaccines (cholera, anthrax, tetanus toxoid), and chloramphenicol, zidovudine, roflumilast and strontium chloride Sr-89.
  - Many of these are immunosuppressants, myelosuppressive agents or vaccines. Combined use with ofatumumab warrants caution, especially in a CLL/SLL population, which is already immunocompromised.

For warnings and contraindications, please refer to the SmPC.
