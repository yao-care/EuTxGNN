## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available from DrugBank (data gap DG002). Based on information available in the clinical trial evidence within this pack, tagraxofusp (also known as SL-401) is a recombinant fusion protein combining human interleukin-3 with a truncated diphtheria toxin, engineered to kill CD123-expressing malignant cells; it is referenced as approved for treatment of BPDCN.

Esotropia is a form of strabismus caused by extraocular muscle or neuromuscular dysfunction. There is no established biological relationship between CD123 expression, diphtheria-toxin-mediated cytotoxicity, and ocular muscle alignment disorders.

No clinical trials or publications were identified to support this indication, and the recommendation is accordingly **Hold**.
