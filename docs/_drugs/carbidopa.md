## Safety Considerations

Label-based warnings and contraindications were not available in the data compiled for this report. Please refer to the SmPC for safety information.

- **Contraindication signals from the knowledge graph (not label text):** The TxGNN knowledge graph links carbidopa to contraindication-type relationships with several disease groups. These include psychiatric disorders (psychotic, schizoaffective, anxiety, and dysthymic disorders), cardiovascular conditions (myocardial infarction, coronary thrombosis, sudden cardiac arrest, heart conduction disease, orthostatic hypotension), and hepatic, renal, and gastrointestinal diseases (liver failure, kidney disease, peptic and gastric ulcer, gallbladder disease). They also include epilepsy, open-angle glaucoma, diabetes and diabetic ketoacidosis, and melanoma. These signals should be verified against the official label.
- **Drug interactions:** The only interaction record is pharmacological. Carbidopa targets L-aromatic amino-acid decarboxylase (DDC), which is its intended mechanism and not a drug-drug interaction.
