## Icatibant: From Hereditary Angioedema to C1 Inhibitor Deficiency
{: .text-alpha }

## One-Sentence Summary

Icatibant is a bradykinin B2 receptor antagonist, originally used to treat acute attacks of hereditary angioedema (HAE) with C1-esterase-inhibitor deficiency.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **C1 inhibitor deficiency**. This is essentially an on-label use rather than true repurposing, supported by **11 clinical trials** and **20 publications**.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the compiled data. Based on known information, icatibant is a selective bradykinin B2 receptor antagonist (a peptide drug that also binds the B1 receptor in pharmacology records). Its efficacy in HAE attacks is established.

C1 inhibitor deficiency causes unregulated kallikrein-kinin activity and excess bradykinin, which drives angioedema attacks. By blocking the B2 receptor, icatibant directly targets the mediator of these attacks. The predicted indication matches the approved label (HAE with C1-esterase-inhibitor deficiency), so it is an on-label use rather than a new indication.

The literature also describes acquired C1 inhibitor deficiency as a bradykinin-mediated disease. A small real-world observational study (8 patients) reported icatibant use in this setting, which supports a possible extension to the acquired form.

## Safety Considerations

- **Drug Interactions**: Moderate-level interactions are listed with ACE inhibitors (benazepril, captopril, enalapril, perindopril, trandolapril, moexipril, lisinopril, quinapril, ramipril, fosinopril). ACE inhibitors raise bradykinin levels, so this combination should be reviewed carefully.

For other safety information, please refer to the SmPC.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Proceed with Guardrails**

**Rationale:**
Several completed Phase 3 trials, including randomized, placebo-controlled and active-comparator studies, support icatibant for HAE with C1 inhibitor deficiency, and the mechanism is direct. Both EU authorizations are listed as cancelled, so regional availability needs confirmation.
