## Safety Considerations

- **Contraindications suggested by the knowledge graph** (derived from drug-disease links, not from label text): kidney disease, liver disease, pancreatitis, gallbladder disease, pituitary hormone deficiencies (including hypopituitarism and Sheehan syndrome), hypogonadotropic hypogonadism, Kallmann syndrome, adrenocortical insufficiency (Addison disease), thymus gland disease, and infectious disease. These need confirmation against the official product information.
- **Drug interactions**: 100 interactions were found, and the 20 listed are mostly moderate. Examples include phenylephrine, pseudoephedrine, epinephrine, salbutamol, formoterol, hydrochlorothiazide, acetazolamide, estradiol, levonorgestrel, norethisterone, hydrocortisone, triamcinolone, benazepril, aripiprazole, apalutamide, alpelisib and ethanol. Topical hydrocortisone and topical alclometasone are minor.
- **Pancreatitis report**: One case report in the literature describes pancreatitis after long-term sitagliptin use (PMID 32337116). This is relevant to the pancreatitis signal above.

Please refer to the SmPC for the full warnings and contraindications.
