## Safety Considerations

- **Drug Interactions**: 14 interaction records were compiled. Thirteen are rated Moderate, and one is a pharmacology target record (CoV Non-structural protein 15) with no severity level. The Moderate interactions are with:
  - Cimetidine
  - Tafenoquine
  - Ketoconazole
  - Brigatinib
  - Crizotinib
  - Encorafenib
  - Ethinylestradiol
  - Lenvatinib
  - Medroxyprogesterone acetate
  - Megestrol acetate
  - Ribociclib
  - Rucaparib
  - Vandetanib

For warnings and contraindications, please refer to the SmPC.
