## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available. Based on known information, guselkumab is an anti-IL-23 monoclonal antibody. Its efficacy in plaque psoriasis, psoriatic arthritis, ulcerative colitis and Crohn's disease is established. Mechanistically, IL-23/IL-17 signaling has been linked to bone remodeling in inflammatory disease, so a role in osteoporosis is conceivable.

However, the data compiled for this report do not support a direct link between IL-23 blockade and drug-induced osteoporosis. Without trial or literature evidence, this connection is speculative, and the model's high score alone is not enough to justify clinical development.

For context, the model's higher-ranked predictions for this drug also include psoriasis and ulcerative colitis. Both are already approved indications and are supported by Phase 3 trials, so they act as positive controls for the model rather than new repurposing findings. The novel predictions (for example diabetic retinopathy and renal osteodystrophy) have no supporting evidence at all.
