## Safety Considerations

- **Predicted disease-level contraindications (from a knowledge graph, not label text)**: hypertriglyceridemia, ileus, intestinal obstruction, vitamin K deficiency hemorrhagic disease, dyskinesia of the esophagus, vitamin D deficiency, osteomalacia, rickets, gastroparesis, familial isolated vitamin E deficiency, and vitamin A deficiency. These reflect the drug's class effects on gut motility and fat-soluble vitamin absorption and should be confirmed against the SmPC.
- **Drug Interactions**: 47 interactions were identified, all rated Moderate in the examples shown. Main ones include:
  - Corticosteroids: hydrocortisone, prednisone, prednisolone, dexamethasone, triamcinolone, budesonide
  - Vitamin D and analogues: cholecalciferol, calcifediol, calcitriol, ergocalciferol, dihydrotachysterol
  - Antidiabetic agents: metformin, glimepiride, glipizide, glyburide, chlorpropamide, acetohexamide
  - Bile acids: chenodeoxycholic acid, cholic acid, obeticholic acid

Please refer to the SmPC for label warnings and formal contraindications.
