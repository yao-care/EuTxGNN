## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on general knowledge, ivacaftor is a CFTR potentiator that increases the open probability of the CFTR channel. Its efficacy in cystic fibrosis with gating mutations is established.

The link to rheumatoid arthritis is weak. A role for CFTR in synovial or immune-cell biology in rheumatoid arthritis is speculative, and no clear mechanistic connection has been identified. The high TxGNN score (0.97) is a graph-based prediction only. It is not supported by any trial or publication in the data compiled for this report, so it should be read as a hypothesis-generating signal, not as evidence of efficacy.

Other top-ranked predictions (HIV infection, leprosy, cytomegalovirus infection, and several rare genetic or developmental syndromes) show the same pattern: high scores, no mechanistic rationale, and no supporting studies. Several of them (feline AIDS, simian immunodeficiency virus infection) appear to be graph artifacts from the HIV-related neighbourhood.
