## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on known information, formoterol is a long-acting beta2-agonist that relaxes airway smooth muscle through cAMP-mediated signalling. Its efficacy in asthma and COPD is established.

The link to respiratory malformation is weak. A bronchodilator can ease airflow obstruction, but it is not expected to correct structural malformations. None of the 9 publications retrieved for this prediction address congenital respiratory malformations. They cover COPD, asthma, Down syndrome cognition, post-COVID small airway disease and allergic bronchopulmonary mycosis. The high score most likely reflects network proximity to formoterol's established bronchodilator use rather than a genuine new-indication signal.

Other predictions for this drug:
- **Obstructive lung disease** (score 99.90%, evidence L1) and **asthma** (score 99.74%, evidence L1) are on-label uses, supported by large Phase 3 programmes.
- **Bronchitis** (score 99.92%) is supported only indirectly, through COPD trials.
- **Rienhoff syndrome** (score 99.90%) and **asthma-related traits, susceptibility to** (score 99.50%) have no trials or literature behind them.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The respiratory malformation prediction rests only on a model score. There are no trials or relevant publications, and a beta2-agonist is not expected to correct structural malformations. The well-supported predictions (obstructive lung disease, asthma) are existing approved uses, not repurposing, and all EU licences are currently cancelled.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Mechanistic evidence for any role of beta2-agonism in respiratory malformation, along with detailed mechanism of action data
- Warnings and contraindications from the SmPC, which are currently unavailable
- Confirmation of current product availability and formulation, given that all EU licences are cancelled
- If an on-label route is pursued, review of the asthma and obstructive lung disease predictions, which could be considered for "Proceed with Guardrails"
