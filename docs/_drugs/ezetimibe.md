# Ezetimibe: From Primary Hypercholesterolaemia to Hyperlipoproteinemia

## One-Sentence Summary

Ezetimibe is a cholesterol-absorption inhibitor, authorised in the EU (as part of the Nustendi combination with bempedoic acid) for primary hypercholesterolaemia, mixed dyslipidaemia and reduction of cardiovascular risk.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **hyperlipoproteinemia**, with **39 clinical trials** and **18 publications** compiled for this prediction. This is essentially an on-label indication rather than a true repurposing candidate.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the compiled data. Based on the analysis compiled for this report, ezetimibe inhibits NPC1L1-mediated intestinal cholesterol absorption, which lowers LDL-C.

Hyperlipoproteinemia is a disorder of elevated blood lipoproteins, and the approved indications (primary hypercholesterolaemia and mixed dyslipidaemia) already cover this space. The predicted indication therefore largely overlaps with the existing label.

Other predictions follow the same pattern. Familial hypercholesterolemia and autosomal dominant hypercholesterolemia are essentially covered by the approved heterozygous familial indication. Other predictions are weaker. HIV-related dyslipidemia has small randomized and pilot studies, but it is treatment of lipid disorders in HIV patients, not antiviral activity. The rare lipid disorders (CYP7A1 deficiency, CETP deficiency, hepatic lipase deficiency) have only indirect or no evidence. Neurodevelopmental disorder, feline immunodeficiency and simian immunodeficiency predictions have no identifiable mechanistic link and look like knowledge-graph artefacts.

## Safety Considerations

- **Disease associations in the knowledge graph (not label text)**: pancreatitis, liver disease, gallbladder disease, myopathy, muscular disease, skeletal muscle disease, familial periodic paralysis, substance abuse/dependence, rhabdomyolysis, nodular fasciitis.
- **Drug Interactions**: 100 interactions were identified. Moderate-level interactions include chenodeoxycholic acid, eluxadoline, rosuvastatin and simvastatin. Others (for example calcitriol, pantoprazole, glimepiride, mesalazine, doxycycline, metformin, omeprazole, morphine, cimetidine, vancomycin and lactulose) have an unclassified severity level.

Please refer to the SmPC for official warnings and contraindications.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Proceed with Guardrails**

**Rationale:**
The top predictions (hyperlipoproteinemia, familial hypercholesterolemia, autosomal dominant hypercholesterolemia) are supported by multiple completed Phase 3 trials and are largely covered by existing indications, so this is effectively on-label use rather than true repurposing. The EU authorisation found is cancelled only, and the lower-ranked predictions (rare lipid enzyme disorders, neurodevelopmental disorder, veterinary or primate immunodeficiency viruses) have no or only indirect evidence and should be held.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Mechanism of action data and SmPC warnings and contraindications (currently unavailable)
- Clarification of the current EU authorisation status for single-agent ezetimibe products
- Review of interactions with statins and with hepatic or muscle-related conditions flagged in the knowledge graph
- For HIV-related dyslipidemia, a dedicated review of interactions with antiretroviral therapy
