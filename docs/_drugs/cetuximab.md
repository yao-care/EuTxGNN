## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Cetuximab is known to target the epidermal growth factor receptor (EGFR), and the pharmacology data confirm EGFR as its target. Its efficacy in EGFR-expressing colorectal and head and neck cancers is established, so it may mechanistically apply to other EGFR-driven tumours.

For the top-ranked prediction, non-seminomatous lesion (non-seminomatous germ cell tumours), the link to EGFR blockade is only speculative. There are no trials or literature, and the very high TxGNN score (0.9995) should not be read as clinical support. Many of the top-ranked predictions share nearly identical scores (about 0.9995), so the score does not distinguish between candidates.

Other predicted indications are more informative:
- **Ductular proliferation (likely pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma):** EGFR signalling is active, but the KRAS mutations present in most of these tumours predict resistance to anti-EGFR antibodies. The literature is mostly preclinical, review or imaging studies, with no clinical efficacy evidence for cetuximab.
- **Cystic neoplasm (likely adenoid cystic and salivary gland carcinoma):** These tumours can express EGFR, and there is a phase II study and a phase I/II trial. Efficacy remains unproven.
- **Pre-malignant neoplasm:** EGFR is overexpressed in premalignant upper aerodigestive lesions, and a completed phase II single-agent cetuximab trial exists.
- **Epiglottis neoplasm:** Epiglottic squamous cell carcinoma falls within head and neck squamous cell carcinoma, which is already an approved indication. This is not a true repurposing candidate.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The top-ranked prediction (non-seminomatous lesion) rests on the model score alone, with no trials, no literature and no plausible EGFR rationale. The best-supported alternatives, cystic neoplasm and pre-malignant neoplasm, reach only L2 and are classified as research questions, not recommendations. In addition, the only EU marketing authorization for Erbitux is cancelled.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Mechanism of action data and a clearer biological rationale linking EGFR to the predicted indication
- Mapping of the vague predicted disease terms (e.g., "cystic neoplasm", "ductular proliferation") to specific diseases
- Results of the completed phase II trial in premalignant upper aerodigestive lesions (NCT00524017)
- Review of the SmPC for warnings and contraindications
- For pancreatic disease, RAS/KRAS status stratification given the expected resistance to anti-EGFR antibodies
- Confirmation of current EU availability, since the marketing authorization is cancelled
