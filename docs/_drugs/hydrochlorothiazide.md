## Safety Considerations

Label-based warnings and contraindications were not available in the data compiled for this report. Please refer to the SmPC for safety information.

- **Knowledge-graph contraindication signals** (from drug-disease relationships in the TxGNN knowledge graph, not label text): liver failure and liver disease, cirrhosis, kidney disease, pancreatitis, gallbladder disease, porphyria, angioedema (including hereditary and RAAS-blocker-induced forms), systemic lupus erythematosus, autoimmune hemolytic anemia, peptic ulcer disease, asthma, bronchitis, epilepsy, anxiety and neurotic disorders, myopathy and familial periodic paralysis.
- **Drug interactions**: 100 interactions were identified. Those listed in the data are mostly Moderate, and include acarbose, rabeprazole, hydrocortisone and other corticosteroids, albiglutide, alogliptin, acetohexamide, amphotericin B (several formulations), bisacodyl, bupropion, and vitamin D analogues (cholecalciferol, calcifediol, calcitriol). Doxycycline and atropine are Minor.
- **Literature safety signals**: case reports of hydrochlorothiazide-associated pulmonary edema and drug-induced methemoglobinemia.
