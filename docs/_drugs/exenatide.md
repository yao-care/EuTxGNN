## Safety Considerations

Drug-disease contraindication links in the knowledge graph (not label text) include:

- **Renal conditions**: impaired renal function, chronic kidney disease, chronic renal failure, end-stage renal failure
- **Gastrointestinal and pancreatic conditions**: pancreatitis, gastroparesis, cholelithiasis, cholecystolithiasis, choledocholithiasis
- **Metabolic and other conditions**: hypoglycemia, hypertensive disorder, hypertension, substance abuse/dependence

**Drug Interactions**: 100 interactions were identified. Most listed ones are of moderate severity, for example:
- Phenylephrine, pseudoephedrine, epinephrine (sympathomimetics)
- Salbutamol, formoterol
- Ketorolac, ibuprofen
- Hydrocortisone, hydrochlorothiazide, acetazolamide
- Estradiol, levonorgestrel, norethisterone
- Amphotericin B, amikacin, amiloride, alpelisib, ethanol

Minor interactions include topical hydrocortisone and topical alclometasone.
