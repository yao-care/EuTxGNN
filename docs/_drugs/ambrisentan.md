## Ambrisentan: From Pulmonary Arterial Hypertension to Pulmonary Arterial Hypertension Associated with Connective Tissue Disease
{: .text-alpha }

## One-Sentence Summary

Ambrisentan is an endothelin receptor antagonist, originally used to treat pulmonary arterial hypertension (PAH) in adults and children.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **PAH associated with connective tissue disease**, with **2 clinical trials** and **19 publications** currently supporting this direction.
This is largely an on-label subgroup, since the approved label already mentions efficacy in PAH associated with connective tissue disease.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the compiled data. Ambrisentan is known to be a selective endothelin type A (ETA) receptor antagonist. Endothelin-1 drives vasoconstriction, smooth muscle proliferation and vascular remodeling in the pulmonary arteries.

Connective tissue disease-associated PAH (CTD-PAH), especially in systemic sclerosis, is a subtype of Group 1 PAH in which endothelin-1 is a key pathogenic mediator. This is the same disease class in which ambrisentan is already approved. The EU label states that efficacy has been shown in PAH associated with connective tissue disease, so this prediction is better understood as confirmation of an on-label subgroup supported by further trial and literature evidence than as a wholly new indication.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Proceed with Guardrails**

**Rationale:**
- CTD-PAH is already named in the approved label, and it is supported by a completed Phase 2 trial, a Phase 4 trial, AMBITION and ARIES-E subgroup analyses, and two meta-analyses. Both trials are small, and the evidence for this subgroup comes mainly from subgroup and post hoc analyses rather than dedicated Phase 3 trials.
- Both EU authorizations are currently cancelled, so any use would need to be checked against current availability of ambrisentan products.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Label-based warnings and contraindications from the SmPC, because the safety screening data are currently missing
- Detailed mechanism of action data from DrugBank
- Confirmation of the current EU market and supply status
- A monitoring plan covering liver function, embryo-fetal toxicity precautions (including pregnancy prevention) and fluid retention
- Review of drug interactions relevant to the patient's concomitant medicines
