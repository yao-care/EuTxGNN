## Safety Considerations

Label-based warnings, contraindications and drug interaction data were not available in the compiled data. Please refer to the SmPC for safety information.

One point from the mechanistic assessment is worth noting: thromboembolic events are a recognised safety concern for luspatercept, and embryo-fetal toxicity was observed in animal studies. Both are relevant to any new use and should be verified against the SmPC.
