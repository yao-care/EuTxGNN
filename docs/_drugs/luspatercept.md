## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Luspatercept is an activin receptor ligand trap (a modified ActRIIB-Fc fusion protein). It inhibits TGF-beta superfamily signalling (Smad2/3) and promotes late-stage red blood cell maturation. This is why it works in anaemias caused by ineffective erythropoiesis, such as MDS and beta thalassaemia. Note that detailed mechanism data was not available in the compiled data; this description comes from the mechanistic assessment prepared for this report.

Monosomy X (Turner syndrome) is a chromosomal disorder characterised mainly by gonadal dysgenesis. There is no established link between this condition and erythroid maturation or the Smad signalling pathway. The high TxGNN score reflects a network-based association only, and **no clear mechanistic rationale supports this prediction**.

The other top-ranked predictions (for example pyruvate kinase deficiency of red cells) have a more plausible biological rationale, but they were not the focus of this report and are also without trial or literature support in the compiled data.
