# Oteracil: From Advanced Gastric Cancer to Colonic Neoplasm

## One-Sentence Summary

Oteracil is a component of the S-1 combination (tegafur/gimeracil/oteracil), originally used to treat advanced gastric cancer and, in certain patients, metastatic colorectal cancer.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be relevant to **colonic neoplasm**, with **6 clinical trials** and **10 publications** currently linked to this direction.
Most of this evidence concerns the S-1 combination as a whole, not oteracil alone, and the colorectal use partly overlaps with the existing EU label.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Oteracil is one of three components of S-1. It inhibits orotate phosphoribosyltransferase (OPRT) and is concentrated in the gastrointestinal mucosa, where it reduces activation of 5-FU and so lowers gastrointestinal toxicity. It has no antitumour activity of its own. Any colorectal benefit therefore comes from the S-1 combination (the tegafur 5-FU prodrug together with gimeracil), not from oteracil alone.

Gastric and colorectal cancers are both gastrointestinal tumours treated with fluoropyrimidines. The EU label for Teysuno already covers metastatic colorectal cancer for patients who cannot continue another fluoropyrimidine because of hand-foot syndrome or cardiovascular toxicity. This prediction is therefore largely an overlap with the existing indication rather than a new repurposing signal. The Phase 3 data in adjuvant stage III colon cancer would extend use beyond the labelled metastatic setting.

Detailed mechanism-of-action data from DrugBank are not currently available, so the mechanistic description above relies on published knowledge of S-1.

## Safety Considerations

Please refer to the SmPC for safety information. Published case reports of S-1-containing regimens describe erythroderma with mucosal involvement, hand-foot syndrome, hypertriglyceridaemia and severe haematological toxicity, which supports toxicity monitoring.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Proceed with Guardrails**

**Rationale:**
A completed Phase 3 RCT (NCT00660894, n=1535) and a large Phase 3 trial of unknown status (NCT03448549) support S-1-based therapy in colon cancer. However, the benefit comes from the S-1 combination rather than oteracil alone, the metastatic setting is already covered by the EU label, and the only EU authorization has been cancelled. Proceeding should be limited to the S-1 combination with careful safety monitoring.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Warnings and contraindications from the SmPC (currently a blocking gap for safety screening)
- Detailed mechanism-of-action data from DrugBank
- Verified results for NCT03448549 and NCT00660894 in the adjuvant stage III colon cancer setting
- Clarification of the regulatory pathway given the cancelled EU authorization
- A safety monitoring plan covering haematological, dermatological and cardiovascular toxicity
