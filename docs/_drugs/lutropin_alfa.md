## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

POTS, in contrast, is primarily a disorder of autonomic nervous system regulation and cardiovascular autonomic reflexes. This candidate should therefore be treated as a low-confidence, exploratory signal rather than a mechanistically grounded repurposing hypothesis.
