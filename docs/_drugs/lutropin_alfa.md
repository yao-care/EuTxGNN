# Lutropin alfa: From Infertility Treatment (Severe LH and FSH Deficiency) to Postural Orthostatic Tachycardia Syndrome

## One-Sentence Summary

Lutropin alfa is a recombinant luteinising hormone (LH), originally used together with an FSH preparation to stimulate follicular development in women with severe LH and FSH deficiency.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **postural orthostatic tachycardia syndrome (POTS)**, but **no clinical trials and no publications** currently support this prediction.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available. Based on known information, lutropin alfa is a recombinant form of LH that acts on LH/hCG receptors in the gonads. Its efficacy in stimulating follicular development in LH-deficient women is the basis of its original approval.

No established mechanistic link to POTS has been identified. The suggestion that hormones may influence autonomic function (POTS is more common in women, and symptoms may vary with the menstrual cycle) is speculative and is not supported by any data compiled for this report. The high TxGNN score (97.04%) reflects a knowledge-graph prediction only and should not be read as biological or clinical validation.

Other top-ranked predictions show the same pattern: peptic esophagitis, trichotillomania, Raynaud disease, duodenal ulcer, Tourette syndrome, esophageal disease, sinoatrial block, multiple endocrine neoplasia and duodenogastric reflux. All scored between 94.6% and 97.0%. All are evidence level L5, with no plausible mechanism, no trials and no literature. For multiple endocrine neoplasia, stimulating endocrine tissue with a gonadotropin could even be a theoretical safety concern.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**

- Safety information (warnings and contraindications) from the product information, which is currently missing and blocks safety screening
- Preclinical or observational evidence supporting the prediction
- Clarification of product availability, given that the only EU authorization is cancelled
