## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Baricitinib is generally known as a JAK1/JAK2 inhibitor, which is how it acts in immune-mediated diseases such as rheumatoid arthritis, atopic dermatitis and alopecia areata.

The predicted indication is a rare developmental syndrome affecting the eye and skeleton. No JAK-STAT-related mechanism is evident for it. The very high score (0.999) most likely reflects artifacts of the knowledge graph rather than a real biological link. The prediction is therefore best regarded as unverified, and it should not be treated as a credible repurposing signal without further evidence.

For context, other predictions for this drug have more plausible mechanistic rationale, for example plasma cell myeloma and indolent plasma cell myeloma (IL-6/JAK/STAT3 signaling). Even these have almost no supporting evidence so far.
