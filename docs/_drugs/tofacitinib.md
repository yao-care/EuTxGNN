## Cytotoxicity

Tofacitinib is a targeted immunomodulatory kinase inhibitor, not a conventional cytotoxic chemotherapy, and its original indications are not oncologic. This section is therefore not applicable.

## Safety Considerations

- **Drug Interactions:** 100 interactions were identified in the DDI screening; the examples below are from the first 20 entries returned.
  - **Major:** Hydrocortisone, triamcinolone (including ophthalmic), dexamethasone, betamethasone, budesonide, prednisone, prednisolone, clarithromycin
  - **Moderate:** Aprepitant, morphine (including liposomal), acetylsalicylic acid, omeprazole, esomeprazole, lansoprazole, miconazole, opium
  - **Minor:** Zinc sulfate, zinc acetate
- **Malignancy concern:** The literature compiled for this report notes that JAK inhibitors carry a malignancy warning. One case report describes chronic myeloid leukaemia arising in a patient receiving tofacitinib. This is a theoretical concern for a haematologic malignancy indication.

Please refer to the SmPC for full warnings and contraindications.
