## Sirolimus: Liposarcoma

## One-Sentence Summary

Sirolimus is an mTOR inhibitor used in transplant medicine and for rare mTOR-driven diseases. The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **liposarcoma**, supported by **1 completed single-arm Phase 2 trial** and a small set of mostly indirect publications. The evidence level is **L3**, because the only study is a single-arm trial and there is no randomized trial.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism-of-action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Sirolimus is known as an mTOR inhibitor, and mTOR signalling (the PI3K/AKT/mTOR pathway) is frequently dysregulated in soft tissue sarcomas, including liposarcoma. Preclinical mouse models of sarcoma have shown that mTOR inhibition can slow tumour growth, which gives a plausible biological reason for testing sirolimus in this disease.

The supporting evidence is limited. The one registered trial combines sirolimus with cyclophosphamide in myxoid liposarcoma and chondrosarcoma, and no efficacy results are available in the data compiled. The literature is mostly reviews of targeted sarcoma therapy and studies of cancer risk in transplant recipients, which only support the idea indirectly. A transplant study (PMID 16434506) suggests that sirolimus-based regimens may reduce cancer risk, but this is not evidence of benefit in liposarcoma.

## Cytotoxicity

Sirolimus is not a conventional cytotoxic agent in its approved uses, and no cytotoxicity data is available in the data compiled. Please refer to the SmPC warnings and precautions.

## Safety Considerations

- **Drug Interactions**: 100 interactions were found in the data compiled; the examples listed are only a sample. Major-level interactions include amphotericin B (including the lipid complex), mesalazine, balsalazide and clarithromycin. Moderate-level interactions include several diabetes medicines (acarbose, metformin, pioglitazone, alogliptin, canagliflozin, dapagliflozin), corticosteroids (hydrocortisone, dexamethasone, betamethasone, budesonide, triamcinolone), aprepitant and cimetidine.

Please refer to the SmPC for warnings and contraindications.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The evidence level is L3: there is one completed single-arm Phase 2 trial (NCT02821507) with no outcome data available, no randomized trial, and the literature is mainly indirect. The mTOR rationale is plausible but not yet backed by demonstrated benefit in liposarcoma.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Published efficacy and safety results from NCT02821507
- Mechanism-of-action data for sirolimus in liposarcoma subtypes
- A comparative or randomized study in liposarcoma
- Full review of the SmPC warnings, contraindications and a drug-interaction management plan
