# Thiotepa: From Haematopoietic Stem Cell Transplant Conditioning to Adult Germ Cell Tumor

## One-Sentence Summary

Thiotepa is an alkylating chemotherapy agent, originally authorised in the EU as part of conditioning treatment before haematopoietic progenitor cell transplantation (HPCT) and for high-dose chemotherapy with HPCT support in solid tumours.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **adult germ cell tumor**, with **15 clinical trials** and **20 publications** linked to this direction. Only a few of these are specific to germ cell tumors, and the indication is close to an existing labelled use, so this is better seen as an extension than a true repurposing.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Thiotepa is known as an alkylating agent that cross-links DNA, and its efficacy in high-dose conditioning regimens is established.

Germ cell tumors are highly sensitive to chemotherapy. Patients with poor-risk or relapsed disease can be salvaged with high-dose chemotherapy and autologous stem cell rescue. Thiotepa has been used in such regimens, for example combined with carboplatin and etoposide, or with cyclophosphamide and carboplatin.

Because thiotepa is already labelled for high-dose chemotherapy with HPCT support in solid tumours, the new-indication novelty is limited. The prediction mostly reflects an existing indication class applied to a specific tumour type. Other predicted entries (various yolk sac tumor histologic patterns, which have no trials or literature) appear to be ontology-derived and are not evaluated further here.

## Safety Considerations

- **Disease associations flagged by the knowledge graph** (not label text): kidney disease, pyoureter, liver disease, gallbladder disease, thrombocytopenia, blood coagulation disease, anaemia, macrocytic anaemia, leukopenia.
- **Drug Interactions**: 100 interactions were recorded. Those with a stated severity include:
  - Major: deferiprone, samarium (153Sm) lexidronam
  - Moderate: bupropion, palifermin, strontium chloride Sr-89, fostamatinib
  - Minor: levofloxacin
  - Many others (for example warfarin, heparin, dexamethasone, prednisone, omeprazole, ondansetron, amphotericin B and vancomycin) have no assigned severity level.

Label-based warnings and contraindications were not available in the data compiled, so please refer to the SmPC for full safety information.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Proceed with Guardrails**

**Rationale:**
Thiotepa-based high-dose regimens with stem cell rescue have published experience in relapsed or refractory germ cell tumors, and the drug is already labelled for high-dose chemotherapy with HPCT support in solid tumours. However, the evidence is mostly single-arm, small, or mixed-population, with no confirmed completed Phase 3 trials. The EU authorisations are currently cancelled, and label safety data are missing.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Current EU authorisation status and the SmPC warnings and contraindications
- Mechanism of action data
- Germ cell tumor-specific efficacy data for thiotepa-containing regimens (for example, a review of the thiotepa-specific studies above)
- A safety and monitoring plan for profound myelosuppression, organ function, and the major drug interactions listed above
