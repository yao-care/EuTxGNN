# Cinacalcet: From Secondary Hyperparathyroidism to Multiple Endocrine Neoplasia

## One-Sentence Summary

Cinacalcet is a calcimimetic originally used to treat secondary hyperparathyroidism in dialysis patients, and to reduce hypercalcaemia in parathyroid carcinoma and primary hyperparathyroidism.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **multiple endocrine neoplasia (MEN)**, with **1 clinical trial** (indirect) and **19 publications** currently supporting this direction.
The evidence is mostly observational and concerns MEN1-related hyperparathyroidism, so it largely extends the approved primary hyperparathyroidism use.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. From known class pharmacology, cinacalcet is an allosteric modulator of the calcium-sensing receptor (CaSR). It makes the receptor more sensitive to extracellular calcium, which lowers parathyroid hormone (PTH) and serum calcium.

In multiple endocrine neoplasia type 1 (MEN1), primary hyperparathyroidism is the most common and earliest manifestation, and it drives hypercalcaemia. Surgery often fails to cure it durably because of high recurrence and repeated neck explorations. Cinacalcet acts through the same mechanism as in its approved primary hyperparathyroidism indication, so it may offer a medical alternative.

Published MEN1 cohorts and case series report calcium-lowering responses. A French multicentre experience also describes off-label use in paediatric primary hyperparathyroidism. No MEN1-specific randomized controlled trial is included in the data.

## Safety Considerations

- **Disease associations from the knowledge graph** (not label text): epilepsy, liver disease, gallbladder disease, hypotensive disorder, hypercalcaemia, nephrocalcinosis, phosphorus metabolism disease, hypophosphataemia.
- **Drug Interactions**: 100 interactions were recorded. Major: eliglustat. Moderate: clarithromycin, dexfenfluramine, eluxadoline, glycerol phenylbutyrate, metoclopramide, miconazole.
- **Published literature signals**: hypocalcaemia and QT prolongation, particularly in paediatric use.

Please refer to the SmPC for warnings and contraindications.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Proceed with Guardrails**

**Rationale:**
Several observational MEN1 cohorts support cinacalcet's calcium-lowering effect, using the same CaSR mechanism as its approved primary HPT use. However, there is no MEN1-specific RCT, and the sole registered trial is not MEN-specific. All EU authorizations are currently cancelled.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Confirmation of product availability, given the "Cancelled only" EU status
- SmPC warnings and contraindications, and a documented mechanism of action
- Prospective or controlled data in MEN1-related hyperparathyroidism
- A monitoring plan for hypocalcaemia and QT interval, and review of the interaction list, particularly eliglustat and other CYP-related drugs
