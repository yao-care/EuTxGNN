# Topotecan: From Small Cell Lung Cancer and Ovarian Cancer to Female Breast Carcinoma

## One-Sentence Summary

Topotecan is a topoisomerase I inhibitor chemotherapy originally authorized in the EU for relapsed small cell lung cancer, metastatic ovarian cancer, and recurrent cervical cancer. The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **female breast carcinoma**, supported by **3 early-phase clinical trials** and **20 publications**, most of which are reviews or preclinical studies. The only clinical study with efficacy data reported no evidence of meaningful activity.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Topotecan is known as a topoisomerase I inhibitor. It causes DNA damage in rapidly dividing cells, and its efficacy in lung and ovarian cancers is established. Mechanistically, it may be applicable to other rapidly proliferating solid tumours such as breast cancer.

Preclinical work (PMID 37987734) suggests that TOP1 inhibition causes R-loop accumulation and synthetic lethality in MYC-driven cancers. This is relevant to MYC-amplified breast cancer subsets such as triple-negative disease. Topotecan is also a substrate of the BCRP/ABCG2 efflux transporter, which is expressed in breast cancer cells and may drive resistance (PMIDs 10930538, 25236865).

The clinical signal is weak. An older phase 2 study of continuous infusional topotecan in advanced breast cancer (PMID 9413954) found no evidence of increased efficacy. A pilot study in breast cancer brain metastases (PMID 11455218) exists, but its findings are not summarized in the available data. Overall, the prediction is mechanistically plausible but not supported by clinical efficacy evidence.

## Safety Considerations

- **Disease-level contraindication signals (knowledge graph, not label text)**: thrombocytopenia, neutropenia, agranulocytosis, anaemia, macrocytic anaemia, interstitial lung disease, diarrhoeal disease, impaired renal function, pulmonary fibrosis, and post-inflammatory pulmonary fibrosis.
- **Drug Interactions**: 100 interactions were identified. Major-level interactions include **deferiprone** and **samarium (153Sm) lexidronam**. Moderate-level interactions include eliglustat, rolapitant, iobenguane (I-131), palifermin, strontium chloride Sr-89, and ibritumomab tiuxetan. Minor-level interactions include levofloxacin and caffeine. Several others are of unknown severity, including morphine, omeprazole, ondansetron, and dexamethasone.

Label-based warnings and contraindications were not available. Please refer to the SmPC for complete safety information.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The TxGNN score is very high (99.92%), but the clinical evidence for breast cancer is weak. The one phase 2 study with efficacy data found no meaningful activity, the three listed trials are early-phase or terminated, and most of the literature consists of reviews or preclinical work. In addition, all EU authorizations for topotecan are currently cancelled, and label safety data could not be retrieved.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Label safety information (warnings and contraindications) from the SmPC
- Mechanism of action data from DrugBank
- Subtype-specific evidence, for example in MYC-driven or triple-negative breast cancer, from preclinical or biomarker-selected studies
- Clarification of the current EU availability of topotecan products, since all three authorizations are cancelled
- Efficacy data from well-designed phase 2 studies in breast cancer
